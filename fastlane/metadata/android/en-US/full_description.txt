Freedom to communicate on your own terms.

Orbit gives you fast, secure, and private messaging and video calls built on Matrix, the open standard for decentralized real-time communication.

Stay connected with friends, family, and communities:
• End-to-end encrypted messaging with zero trackers or data mining
• High-definition 1:1 and group voice & video calling
• Rich messaging: emoji reactions, replies, polls, pinned messages, voice notes, and media editing
• Ephemeral view-once media and secure file sharing
• Public communities and private spaces for your teams and clubs
• Universal interoperability across the Matrix open ecosystem
• Seamless multi-device synchronization

Privacy First & True Ownership
Choose where your data lives — use matrix.org, another public server, or host your own server on your custom domain. You have complete ownership of your conversations.

The application uses the USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT permission to ensure you receive incoming call notifications when your device screen is locked.
